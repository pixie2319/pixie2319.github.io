Privacy Policy

This application does not collect, store, or share data on behalf of any third party. It accesses only the TikTok account of its owner (PixieReigns), solely to publish that owner's own video content. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored locally and are never transmitted to any other party.

Jarvis Calendar (Google Calendar)

A private tool the owner uses to copy their own schedule into one Google calendar named "Jarvis" in their own Google account. It uses the Google Calendar permission that only reaches calendars the tool itself created; it cannot read any other calendar, email, or file. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. No Google data is sold, shared, transferred, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
